About Me

I care about the details because users notice them even when they can't say why.

I got into frontend development because I like the moment when something confusing becomes obvious. A clean layout, a quick page load, and a button that does what you expect all make a product feel trustworthy. That's the kind of work I want to do.

I build websites and web apps with a focus on performance, accessibility, and clear structure. I work well with designers, founders, and engineers, and I explain my decisions in plain language.
