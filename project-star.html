STAR International Preparatory JHS

A credible, structured online home for a junior high school, connecting prospective parents with online admissions, calendar schedules, and student life.

01 // The Challenge

The Problem

Junior high schools face intense competition for enrollment. Parents researching institutions online were met with fragmented social media posts and outdated contact details.

STAR International Preparatory JHS needed an authoritative, trustworthy, and easy-to-navigate online presence where prospective parents could review academic programs, verify institutional credentials, check term dates, and complete admissions applications directly from their smartphones.

02 // The Solution

Key Architectural Features

Engineered a responsive, multi-page platform with zero heavy runtime overhead, ensuring instantaneous loading even on 3G cellular connections in suburban regions.

FEATURE 01

Online Application Pipeline

Structured multi-step enrollment form with real-time field validation, document submission checklists, and applicant status tracking.

FEATURE 02

Interactive School Calendar

Clean term-schedule viewer categorizing academic dates, sports meets, exam weeks, and holidays for seamless parent planning.

FEATURE 03

Student Life Showcase

Curated multimedia sections showcasing co-curricular activities, leadership clubs, and science competitions with accessible image alternatives.

FEATURE 04

Complete Legal Compliance

Four dedicated policy documents (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, Refund Policy) instilling institutional accountability.

03 // Architecture

Pages & Structure

A deliberate 9-page information architecture organized into public engagement and legal verification tiers:

Home (index.html)
About Us (about.html)
Students Life (students.html)
Application (application.html)
Calendar (calendar.html)
Privacy Policy
Terms of Service
Cookie Policy
Refund Policy
04 // Engineering

Tech & Approach

  • Zero Runtime Dependencies: Handcrafted HTML5 and CSS3 avoiding client-side framework overhead for instant Time to Interactive (TTI).
  • GitHub Actions Automation: Configured automated linting, HTML validation, and continuous deployment triggers over 106+ Git commits.
  • Defensive Forms: Client-side validation prevents incomplete admissions filings and guides parents with clear, empathetic error messaging.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance: Enforced 4.5:1 text contrast ratios, accessible form labels (`<label for="...">`), and full keyboard navigation.
05 // Outcomes

The Result

The school transformed its admissions inquiries from fragmented phone calls into a structured, trackable online funnel.
