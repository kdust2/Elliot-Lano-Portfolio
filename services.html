How I Work

A Disciplined, Transparent Process

Every project follows four structured phases to eliminate surprises and guarantee on-time delivery.

01

Discover

We clarify your goals, audience personas, content structure, and technical constraints. I ask the questions that avoid rework later.

02

Design

Establishing typography hierarchies, color contrast tokens, fluid grid scales, and interactive motion rules before writing code.

03

Build

Writing semantic, accessible, and performant code. Iterative milestones with live preview links so you see tangible progress every week.

04

Launch

Cross-browser testing, accessibility audit checks, DNS configuration, and documentation handover so your team stays in control.
